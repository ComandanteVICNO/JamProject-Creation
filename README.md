## About

Welcome to the doodled world of **Bun**, a survivor-like fan who decided to make one for himself.

Pick a colour for Bun, then roll the dice and let fate gear him up. His favourite everyday items, even his matcha, turn deadly here. Survive as long as you can on Bun's colourful canvas.

- **6 weapons** and **6 stats** to roll for, each with its own Bun Card
- Endless runs with dice-based upgrades
- Hand-drawn, doodle-style art

**Theme:** Creation

**Limitation:** Roll the Dice. Every upgrade is decided by a dice roll instead of a free choice.
